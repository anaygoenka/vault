# Vault Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

Vault is a clipboard history app for macOS. It is built to keep what you copy on your Mac and nowhere else.

## What Vault stores

Vault saves the text, links, images, file references and colours you copy, so you can find and reuse them. This history is stored only on your Mac, inside Vault's own app container. It is never uploaded, synced, shared or sold.

## What Vault does not collect

- No accounts, sign-in or personal profile.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or crash reporting.
- No network connections. Vault does not talk to any server.

## Sensitive content

Vault skips anything that password managers mark as private, and it ignores apps you add to its exclusion list. You can pause recording at any time from the menu bar.

## Deleting your data

Delete single clips with ⌘⌫, clear everything from the menu bar, or choose how long history is kept (from one hour to forever). Deleting Vault removes all of its data.

## Permissions

The Mac App Store edition of Vault asks for no permissions. The direct-download edition, Vault Direct, can use Accessibility, if you allow it, only to press ⌘V for you after you choose a clip. Neither edition reads your screen or your keystrokes.
